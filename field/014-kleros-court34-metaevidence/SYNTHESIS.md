# Lead Synthesis — Court 34 MetaEvidence Classification

## FACT — Run-Level

- 135 court-34 disputes classified by MetaEvidence.
- 46 unique policyURI.
- Two policyURIs dominate: 46 + 37 = 83 (61%).
- Both dominant URIs are Spanish-language consumer
  templates.
- Category "Services": 87. Category "Agentic Commerce": 24.
- Category is user-supplied, not court-defined.

## OBSERVATION

- The same policyURI appears with different categories
  in different disputes. Category does not describe
  the template; it describes the user's own framing.
- The 2026-Q3 spike is dominated by two consumer
  templates, not by any Agentic-related product.
- Court 34 is general-purpose but de facto dominated
  by consumer disputes in this period.

## UNKNOWN

  one product, one organization, or multiple users.
- Whether "Agentic Commerce" disputes that do exist
  (24) share a specific operator.
- Why three courts (29, 32, 34) host Argentina-style
  consumer disputes in sequence.

## LEAD INTERPRETATION

The Run 002 External claim that court 34 is the
"Agentic Commerce Court" is contradicted by
MetaEvidence. Court 34 is general-purpose, and its
2026-Q3 spike is driven by Spanish consumer
disputes using two recurring templates.

The word "Agentic" in the category field is used
loosely by some users, but is not a court-level
property. Any External statement that describes
court 34 as Agentic-specific should not be treated
as confirmed.
