# Lead Synthesis — Court 34 Identity

## FACT — Run-Level

- Court 34 carries 135 of 296 disputes total, and
  129 of 146 in 2026-Q3.
- 10 evenly spaced samples show heterogeneous
  dispute types: Image Generation, Services,
  Agentic Commerce, Oracle, prediction_market.
- Category field is user-supplied MetaEvidence data,
  not a court property.
- "Agentic Commerce" appears in 1 of 10 samples.

## OBSERVATION

- Court 34 functions as a general-purpose dispute
  court for DisputeResolver users.
- The distribution is dominated by Spanish-language
  consumer service disputes, but includes other
  types.
- No single product or template dominates the
  observed sample.

## UNKNOWN

- Whether court 34 was used for an Agentic Commerce
  demo in August 2026 and continued in general use.
- Whether the Run 002 External identification
  ("Agentic Commerce Court") was based on an
  official source that has since changed.
- Whether the recent density is a test period or
  regular use.

## LEAD INTERPRETATION

The identification of court 34 as a general-purpose
court weakens but does not fully refute the External
Run 002 claim. Court 34 may have been announced
under a specific name and remained in general use
afterwards.

For Envoy, the operative fact is: court 34 is used
for many different kinds of disputes. It is not
observed as an Agentic-specific venue.

## Decision

The "Agentic Commerce Court" label for court 34 is
not confirmed by the sampled MetaEvidence in this
corpus.
